# Lumen product and roadmap reset design

## Status

Approved through product brainstorming on 2026-09-10. This design replaces device-led delivery sequencing while preserving the implemented Space authority, encrypted storage, Host execution, and Hermes integration foundations.

## Product promise

Lumen is a proprietary paid personal intelligence Space. A user can speak naturally from any supported surface, continue one inspectable relationship and memory, and safely make things happen across permitted devices and services.

Jarvis is the interaction inspiration, not a separate authority or runtime. It means continuous presence, conversational interaction, permitted awareness, cross-node agency, and restraint. Lumen remains the product and Space identity.

## Ownership

The Space Authority owns identity, canonical conversations and accepted memory, capability grants, approvals, durable tasks, automation definitions, audit, encryption, export, and migration. It actively selects and coordinates nodes.

Hermes is Lumen's native intelligence and capability platform. Lumen adopts its agent loop, provider routing, tools, skills, MCP, gateways, browser, voice, vision, automation, delegation, remote environments, plugins, and observability before considering another implementation. Hermes output remains untrusted evidence and Hermes never becomes Space authority.

Nodes expose typed abilities and revalidate authorized invocations locally. A device, integration, model, runtime, tool, relay, and external message are never trusted merely because they are connected.

## Reuse doctrine

For each subsystem, Lumen first adopts Hermes, then extends Hermes through supported interfaces, then benchmarks mature external components for measured gaps, and builds only Space-specific, native-boundary, or otherwise missing behavior. Every upstream dependency records its version, provenance, commercial license, security and maintenance health, data flow, supported environments, benchmarks, fallback, rollback, and kill switch.

An optimization such as Caveman may compress projected prompts or tool output only when it proves semantic quality and net savings. It never transforms canonical Space records, and managed use requires compatible hosted-product rights.

## Experience

The first ten-minute product journey is:

1. Create a managed or self-hosted Space.
2. Start one conversation in the responsive web experience.
3. Connect one messaging surface through a Hermes gateway.
4. Pair one lightweight capability node.
5. Tell Lumen a preference and inspect why it was remembered.
6. Continue from another surface with the same context.
7. Request one restricted cross-node action, inspect the preview, approve it, and receive a durable result.
8. Later receive one useful automation suggestion and decide whether it may earn a narrow grant.

The primary product surfaces are Conversation, Memory, Devices and abilities, Activity, and Automations. Runtime, model, deployment, and integration controls use progressive disclosure for power users and self-hosters.

## Deployment

Combined, separated, managed, and hybrid are supported topologies under one Space contract. Development, personal, and hardened are independent assurance levels.

Canonical storage stays with the Space Authority. A combined deployment places Host, storage, Hermes, gateways, and workers on one machine as separately supervised services. A separated deployment moves Hermes or workers behind pinned mutually authenticated transport. Managed Lumen provisions a dedicated data plane per customer while a shared control plane receives only minimum operational metadata. Hybrid nodes may execute locally and synchronize permitted outcomes.

Managed and paid self-hosted products use the same export format, public interoperability contracts, and conformance requirements.

## Memory and model policy

Hermes may retrieve task-scoped projections and propose memory. The Host may accept low-risk useful memory automatically with provenance, classification, retention, confidence, inspection, correction, and deletion. Sensitive or consequential memory requires confirmation.

Hermes may optimize model quality, latency, and cost only inside Lumen-approved provider, model, region, retention, locality, budget, and data-class constraints.

## Presence and autonomy

Wake-word and voice-activity detection run locally and emit no audio or activation metadata before successful activation. The shared presence vocabulary is `idle`, `wake_detected`, `listening`, `understanding`, `thinking`, `acting`, `approval_needed`, `speaking`, `interrupted`, `offline`, and `degraded`. Voice supports barge-in and one cancellation lineage through capture, inference, actions, and playback.

Lumen is suggestive before it is autonomous. Trust graduates through previewed, repeated successful workflows into explicit narrow revocable grants. Delegated or scheduled work cannot expand the parent capability, context, credential, target, provider, budget, deadline, or cancellation scope.

## Commercial and public boundary

Lumen's product implementation is proprietary and paid. Managed hosting and self-hosted deployment are commercial offerings. Public interoperability includes node and capability protocols, schemas, SDKs, conformance fixtures, and compatibility policy. Public protocols do not grant use of Lumen trademarks, hosted services, proprietary implementation, or commercial entitlements.

## Delivery and acceptance

Delivery follows the capability-first milestones in [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md). Hardware and platform apps are reference adapters, not roadmap organizers. Each milestone ships one real journey and passes acceptance, negative, recovery, privacy, operational, compatibility, upgrade, and rollback checks.

Public launch requires demonstrated continuity across web, messaging, voice, and a capability node; transparent memory; safe cross-node action; managed/self-hosted portability; earned autonomy; and sustained real-user reliability and retention.
